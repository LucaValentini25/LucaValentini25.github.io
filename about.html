about.md

Systems and tools programmer.

Senior Unity Developer with 6+ years in Unity and C#, building and shipping games across PC and mobile in studio teams since 2023. I work best architecting structured, self-contained game systems, and building the custom tooling that raises the whole team's productivity. Toolmaking is what I enjoy most.

From January 2025 to September 2026 I was at KYKUYO on SOLAR, a live geolocation/AR mobile game, where I owned the character dressing system, the camera-context architecture, the onboarding flow and the in-app purchase store. I also took the technical direction of SPCA × SOLAR, because I was the one carrying it through to delivery.

I led the technical direction of Waxy: The Wick and the Witch at 3AM Games Studio, and I teach Artificial Intelligence and pathfinding at Escuela Da Vinci, where I graduated.

Most of my recent work is done with coding agents: a game jam entry, a multiplayer netcode project, and features I shipped on a live mobile title. The architecture decisions and the quality bar stay with me.
