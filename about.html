Background

Senior Unity Developer with 6+ years building and shipping games across PC and mobile. I work best as a systems and tools programmer: architecting structured, self-contained game systems rather than one-off gameplay, and building the custom tooling that raises the whole team's productivity.

Toolmaking is what I most enjoy — I actively look for ways to automate and simplify repetitive work, both inside Unity and outside it when the problem calls for it. I also publish my Unity tools as open UPM packages, and one of them runs in a shipped commercial game.

Currently I'm Senior Unity Developer at KYKUYO, working on SOLAR, a live geolocation/AR mobile game on Google Play and the App Store. There I own the character dressing system, the camera-context architecture, the onboarding flow and the in-app purchase store. I also took the technical direction of SPCA × SOLAR, a time-limited campaign game, as its lead programmer.

Alongside that I led the technical direction of Waxy: The Wick and the Witch, which won the ADVA MVP mentorship program and was rebuilt from the ground up into a market-ready title, where I led programming and built most of its custom editor tooling.

I also teach at Escuela Da Vinci, the university where I graduated — first as a teaching assistant helping students plan and architect the systems behind their thesis projects, and now as a professor of Artificial Intelligence and pathfinding algorithms.

Outside work I build to keep learning — most recently Brain App, a local-first AI assistant with a layered architecture and a suite of 323 test cases that runs green in 77 seconds, where the LLM only classifies intent while deterministic code does the work.
